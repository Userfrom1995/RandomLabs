# Choreography spine: exchange-by-exchange fight script

One readable exchange per beat (legibility rule). Each entry: who advances,
who is hit, what it costs, how the storm answers.

- b01 Terms Spoken: no exchange. Weapons grounded. Cost: none. Storm: level 1,
  distant surf-bed only.
- b02 Ranging Wind: footwork only. Thor circles sunwise, Zeus mirrors. Feints at
  18+9s and 18+16s, no contact. Cost: fatigue 0 to 1 each. Storm answers with a
  cross-gust at the second feint (wind force 0.45).
- b03 First Exchange: Thor advances (overhead haft swing, apex at 40+6s); Zeus
  shaft-blocks high (impact frame 40+6.5s, spark-spray anchored at the crossing
  point); Thor overextends half a step and yields ground. Cost: Thor fatigue 2,
  pride dented. One exchange only; no follow-up flurry.
- b04 Thrown Weapons: wager beat. Zeus throws first (60+3s), Thor answers
  (60+5s); weapons cross mid-sky over the crag; sky-crack at 60+9s; both catch
  clean (60+14s, 60+16s). Cost: both exposed mid-recall, neither exploits it;
  mutual respect recorded in the ledger. Storm rises to 3.
- b05 Body Blows: clinch. Thor drags Zeus to shoulder range; traded body blows
  (82+6s, 82+11s); Zeus takes a knee (82+15s) and rises (82+18s); shaft dropped,
  honestly lost, not teleported. Cost: Thor split brow, Zeus bruised ribs,
  fatigue 5 each.
- b06 The Storm's Answer: no duel. Skyburst breaks over the crag (106+4s);
  both go back to back and weather it; weapons set down, not vanished. Cost:
  shared exhaustion (fatigue 6). Storm peaks at 4, then spends itself.
- b07 The Final Exchange: one clean opening. Zeus reads Thor's guard (126+10s),
  places the shaft at Thor's brow (126+16s), and stays the blow through a held
  4-second silence. Cost: the debt of mercy. No blood drawn this beat.
- b08 Loosing the Storm: no duel. Both raise open hands (150+8s) and loose the
  storm east out to sea (wind dir flips to 90). Cost: none further. Storm 0.

Impact-frame lattice (24 fps): every impact lands on a frame boundary; SFX and
score accents share the same event clock (Phases 3-4 wire the accents).
