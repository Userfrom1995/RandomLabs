# Music direction: original motif map (score lands Phase 4)

All material is original, synthesized in-repo. No third-party tracks, no
external streaming.

- Thor row (northern drum-and-horn-row): low frame drum ostinato plus a broad
  four-note horn call. Beats: b01 (terms), b05 (body blows).
- Zeus row (Olympian string-and-bronze-row): plucked string figure plus struck
  bronze resonance. Beats: b02 (ranging).
- Clash ostinato: interlocked drum-plus-bronze figure for weapon beats. Beats:
  b03, b04, b07.
- Resolution hymn: unison long tones resolving the clash interval, first hinted
  in b06 (storm weathered together), full statement in b08 (loosing).
- Continuous coverage rule: every beat carries at least one cue resolving to a
  performed voice; no dead air; voiced beats duck the storm beds (measured
  floors land with the mix in Phase 4).
