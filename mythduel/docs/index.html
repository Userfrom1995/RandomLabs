Pipeline

Deterministic sources-to-stage path: story/duel.json declares the bout; the engine is a pure function of (timeline, time) on a 24 fps grid; the player paints it; the tools verify it. bash mythduel/repro.sh verifies the full build: binding audit, headless suites, audio render, poster check, capture, render, and captions rebuild.

Craft

Hand-drawn-style paint pass: layered crag washes, design-faithful FK fighter rigs with gaze, effort faces, exertion tremor and wounds, wind-driven cloth and hair, 12 fps ink boil, seeded paper grain, and geography-anchored impact particles. Every value is quantized to the 24 fps grid, so scrubbing, stepping, trailer cuts, and capture cards all paint identical pixels.

Story

A free storm, a challenged consent, ranging exchanges, a throwing wager, body blows, the sky turning on both fighters, one withheld killing blow, and a shared loosing out to sea. Mercy as strength.
