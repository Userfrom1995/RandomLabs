# Story

A free-roaming storm neither fighter called gathers over the strait between
their far shores. Thor claims the right to loose it; Zeus refuses him the sky.
They meet by challenged consent on the storm-crag with spoken terms: first
blood honourably taken ends it, and the sky goes free whatever falls.

Escalation runs through ranging footwork, a first blocked exchange, a wager of
thrown weapons recalled, and a clinch of body blows that costs Thor a split
brow and Zeus bruised ribs. Then the sky turns on both fighters and must be
weathered back to back. The final exchange offers Zeus one clean opening at
Thor's brow. He stays the blow. Both loose the storm east out to sea together,
where no roof burns and no sail breaks. Mercy as strength; no narrator
explains what the picture already says.
