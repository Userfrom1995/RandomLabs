# Hearthlight Reimagined - Character Bible and Model-Sheet Parameters

Original characters created for this film. No reference to, or derivation
from, any existing studio's characters. All proportions below are the
committed model-sheet parameters that the human rigs in
`film/engine/humans.js` / `film/engine/faces.js` (Phase 2) are built from.
Head-unit system: 1 hu = height of the character's own head.

Cast discipline: four speaking humans (Nia, Yara, Tam, Lumi) plus Ruel in one
supporting appearance plus the wordless wind. The Narrator is a caption voice
only, never staged. No other speaking parts. Villagers in the rekindling wave
are distant silhouettes. No animal sidekicks, no comic-relief props.

## Nia - keeper-in-training, 12 (lead)

- **Role:** Protagonist. Lets the hill lantern fail on her watch, earns back
  trust by carrying one keeper flame and two children to the high cairn, and
  pays the film's costly choice with half the flame. Arc: hasty reader of
  wind becomes steady keeper.
- **Silhouette:** Small sturdy frame, big patched cloak that bellies in wind
  (primary acting surface), survey pole taller than she is, ribbon cluster at
  the shoulder, keeper's lantern carried low at the hip from s06 on.
  Readable at 390 px by cloak triangle + pole line + lantern dot alone.
- **Model sheet:** Height 4.5 hu. Head: round, charcoal hair cut short with a
  windswept tuft (3 spikes, always leeward). Eyes: large, amber, single ink
  line upper lid, catchlight only when the flame is near. Cloak: rust-red
  (#a03a2a) with one patched elbow square; hem lifts with wind strength
  0..1. Scarf: storm-blue with Yara's knotted ribbon (added s08). Boots:
  dark, mud-flecked from s09 on.
- **Turnaround:** Front / three-quarter / profile / back. Profile: nose a
  single short stroke, chin tucked when carrying. Back view: cloak closes to
  a bell, ribbon knot visible at the collar. Left-right symmetry pinned
  within 2% on shoulders, eyes, and boots (craft test).
- **Expression sheet:** Joy (s09, open mouth, cheeks up), fear (s02/s13,
  stillness, lids wide, mouth a short line), grief (s02/s17, brows in,
  mouth downturned, no tears drawn except s14 reflection), resolve (s06/s11,
  chin down, brows level), exhaustion (s16, lids at half, head tipped to the
  count). Every expression must render on the phoneme base without breaking
  the mouth shapes for her dialogue beats.
- **Acting notes:** Thinks with her hands (charting gestures); fear shows in
  stillness, not shaking; smiles twice (s09 at the shepherd, s11 at Lumi's
  correction); cries without sound in s14, flame reflected in eyes.
- **Voice (captions):** Short sentences, wind vocabulary. Never says what the
  picture already says.

## Grandmother Yara - retired keeper, 78 (lead)

- **Role:** Mentor and threshold guardian. Withholds trust in Act 1, sets the
  price in Act 2, waits at home through Acts 3-4, repays trust without a word
  in the coda.
- **Silhouette:** Tall stoop, shawl draped to a peak, keeper's staff she no
  longer needs but keeps by the door. Hands large and steady (close-up
  acting surface in s06).
- **Model sheet:** Height 4.0 hu standing (5.2 hu un-stooped in the s07
  memory beat only). Hair: silver bun with two lacquer sticks. Shawl:
  indigo (#33406a) with embroidered wind-rose at the back hem. Eyes: pale
  grey, heavy calm lids; gaze always arrives before her head turns.
- **Turnaround:** Front / three-quarter / profile / back, leaning on the
  staff in all four. The wind-rose embroidery is visible only in the back
  view: a reward for the audience, never dialogue.
- **Expression sheet:** Calm (baseline, lids low), tenderness (s06/s08, lids
  lower, mouth soft), resolve (s05, spine straightens half a head-unit),
  grief withheld (s04, hands still, face unchanged: the stillness IS the
  beat). Yara never hurries and never raises her voice; her farewell (s08)
  is played entirely in the hands knotting the ribbon.
- **Secondary motion:** Shawl hem and bun sticks drift at half Nia's
  keyframe rate; stillness is her power.

## Tam - ferryman's son, 17 (lead)

- **Role:** Deuteragonist. Civil, frightened, competent at boats, useless on
  cliffs. Guards Lumi with an oar, belays the traverse, carries her up the
  storm slope. Arc: boy who names every danger becomes the man who climbs
  past them counting aloud.
- **Silhouette:** Long-limbed, river-wading build; oilskin vest over a
  darned sweater; oar carried across the shoulders like a yoke until s12,
  then left at the gorge. Readable at 390 px by the shoulder-yoke line.
- **Model sheet:** Height 5.5 hu (tallest of the party, a full head over
  Nia). Head: narrow, hair river-dark tied back with twine. Eyes: deep
  brown, brows heavy. Vest: tar-dark (#3a3630) with rope-burn scoring on the
  left shoulder (earned in s12). Sweater: undyed wool (#b8a888). Bare feet
  wrapped in cloth from s13 on (boots lost to the mud in s09's crossing).
- **Turnaround:** Front / three-quarter / profile / back. Profile: the oar
  yoke must clear the head by half a head-unit in every pose. Back view:
  sweater darning in a visible cross pattern, Lumi's small hand often at
  his elbow (blocking note, not geometry).
- **Expression sheet:** Fear (s10/s12, jaw set, eyes on the danger, never on
  the camera), shame (s11, gaze down, oar lowered), grief (s13, mouth open
  on Lumi's name), wonder (s15, the one unguarded face in the film), awe
  (s17, head tipped back to the lit cairn).
- **Acting notes:** Leads with the shoulder, not the chin. All courage is
  played as counterpoint: hands busy (knots, belays, carries) while the face catches
  up a beat later.
- **Voice (captions):** River vocabulary, short warnings, counts aloud when
  frightened (the s12/s16 counts are the same tic, repurposed).

## Lumi - ferryman's daughter, 8 (lead, the truth-teller)

- **Role:** The film's truth-teller. Carried for half the crossing with a
  bound ankle, observant, correcting Nia's rope count and naming the choice
  before Nia can dodge it ("Do not spend it"). Ties the final ribbon.
- **Silhouette:** Smallest of the party, carried high (on Tam's back s13,
  in Nia's arms s14); bound ankle wrapped in white that reads at any size;
  hair in two knots that bounce on a two-frame delay (secondary-motion
  showcase).
- **Model sheet:** Height 3.5 hu. Head: large and round (younger
  proportion), hair ember-brown in two topknots. Eyes: largest of the cast,
  dark gold. Tunic: moss-green (#5a7048) over rust leggings; ankle binding
  white (#e8e2d4), re-wrapped tighter in s14 (continuity beat).
- **Turnaround:** Front / three-quarter / profile / back. Carried poses are
  separate plates: back-carry (s13), arm-carry (s14), walking with a stick
  (s19 coda: the ankle healed, the stick a souvenir).
- **Expression sheet:** Wonder (s10, whole face open), resolve (s11/s12,
  chin up, the "got you" face), exhaustion (s14/s16, lids heavy but gaze
  held on Nia), joy (s18/s19, the film's widest smile, reserved for the
  payoff). Lumi never cries: pain goes to the hands (gripping) while the
  face stays brave, which is why the audience cries instead.
- **Acting notes:** Stillness is hers too, but watchful rather than fearful.
  Every Lumi line is short; the face finishes the sentence.
- **Voice (captions):** Plain words, no wind or river vocabulary. Says what
  adults circle around.

## Ruel - the mossback boar, ancient (supporting)

- **Role (demoted from the #449 cut):** Guardian of the gorge in one
  supporting appearance (s12 only). Hauls the spare rope, delivers the
  film's single dry joke, witnesses the traverse from below. Never carries
  an emotional beat a human should carry.
- **Silhouette:** Massive quadruped, shoulder hump crowned with moss and
  lichen-antler branches, ember-orange eyes in deep shadow. Readable at any
  size by hump + antler rake.
- **Model sheet:** Shoulder height 2.1x Nia's full height; length 3.4x Nia.
  Hide: slate-brown (#4a4038) with moss-green (#5a7048) mantle thickest on
  the hump. Lichen antlers: pale sage, 5 tines each side, asymmetrical.
  Tusks: short, chipped left. Breath-mist particle emitter in cold shots.
- **Acting notes:** Speaks once, slowly, with dry humor. All emotion in the
  ears; the face barely moves. Footfalls land on the score's downbeats in
  s12 (sync point, retained from the #449 cut).

## The valley wind - a presence, not a person

- **Role:** Antagonist turned guide. Adversary in Acts 1-4, ally in s15,
  celebrant in Act 5. Never anthropomorphized: no face, no voice.
- **Design language:** Rendered only through its effects: ribbon angles,
  reed bends, snow spirals, spray vectors, ember drift. One consistent
  vector field per shot. The turn in s15 is the film's midpoint miracle and
  must read purely visually before the narrator confirms it.
- **Leitmotif pairing:** The wind motif (score) is its only "face"; when the
  motif turns to major in s15, the audience understands before Nia does.
