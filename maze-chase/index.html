Maze Chase (Rust)

An original maze-chase game in Rust: one deterministic engine with three full publication layers (terminal, desktop, browser). You are Pip, a hungry courier lantern; outrun the four wardens, clear every crumb, and turn the tables with a sunseed.

Status: Phase 1 (research and legal foundations) complete. No playable build exists yet: the engine, assets, and all three layers are planned and tracked in the feature matrix. There is nothing to download or run in this phase, and this page offers no play button until the WASM layer ships in Phase 7.

Layers (all planned)

  • Terminal: installable package, fullscreen canvas, Kitty/Sixel/Unicode tiers with auto-degrade, dynamic resize.
  • Desktop: windowed plus fullscreen GUI on the shared renderer core, keyboard plus gamepad, per-OS packaging.
  • Browser: same renderer as WebAssembly with keyboard plus touch, served from this hub.
