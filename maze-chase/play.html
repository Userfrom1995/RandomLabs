Maze Chase

You are Rill, a pond frog. Clear every crumb, dodge the wardens Bram, Kessa, Jory and Nib, and grab a sunseed to turn the hunt around.

Start game: begin a hunt in the pond.

Wardens: Bram the heron, Kessa the pike, Jory the snake, and Nib the dragonfly.

Clear 445 crumbs plus 4 sunseeds; sunseeds turn the hunt.

How to play

Move with arrows or WASD (remappable in Settings). Eat all crumbs to clear the level. Sunseeds grant fright powers: eat wardens for chain bonuses. Relishes appear for bonus points. P or Esc pauses; M mutes.

Modes: Classic, Time Attack, Sprint, Fright Frenzy. Difficulty presets (Cozy, Standard, Harsh) pick the start level and mode.
