# Hearthlight Reimagined - Full Story and Animation Rebuild with Proper Human Characters

A complete story-and-craft rebuild of the Hearthlight short film at `/film/`, ordered as the owner's follow-up verdict on the closed #449 epic. The #449 premiere cut stays as the historical record; this blueprint designs its successor: a coherent 4-5 minute original story a first-time viewer can follow, led by properly designed and animated human characters with an original visual identity, set in natural detailed painted backgrounds, delivered with true hand-drawn-feel motion, believable dialogue delivery, and continuous sound and score. Same Pages theatre contract, same reproducible pipeline, strictly higher craft bar.

## Logline (rebuild target, Builder to finalize in the new screenplay)

When keeper-in-training Nia lets the valley's last lantern fail on her watch, she must earn back her grandmother Yara's trust by guiding a stranded young ferryman and his injured sister through a storm-wracked gorge to relight the high cairn before the dark month closes the pass - and Nia must choose whose light to spend when one flame cannot save them all.

The logline above is a starting constraint, not the script: it forces what #449 lacked - a legible goal, a ticking clock, a human relationship triangle with conflicting needs, a costly choice, and an ending earned by character change rather than spectacle.

## Deliverables

- **The rebuilt film** - a real, playable 4-5 minute short (`film/index.html` on Pages): deterministic real-time canvas render of a fully rewritten timeline with an original synthesized orchestral score, re-timed dialogue captions, and chapter navigation. Every player control keeps working under the existing contract: play/pause, scrub/seek, chapters, captions toggle, fullscreen, volume/mute, honest loading/error states, reduced-motion respect, 390 px mobile verified.
- **New story package** - logline, full rewritten screenplay with timed shot list and cause-effect continuity ledger, character bible with the new human leads (model sheets, expression sheets, turnarounds), storyboard panels regenerated from the new cut, music direction revised to the new beats, all committed under `film/story/`. Old #449 sources are replaced in place; no parallel film directory, no versioned fork of the theatre.
- **Proper human characters** - two to three fully designed human leads with an original visual identity (never copying any existing studio's assets): proportion-accurate bodies, articulated faces (eyes, brows, mouths with viseme-grade phoneme shapes for dialogue beats), hands capable of holding, giving, and lighting, costume with secondary motion. Non-human cast (Ruel-class creature, wind presence) is supporting only and never carries an emotional beat a human should carry.
- **Painted world** - a natural, detailed background paint set covering every location in the new screenplay: layered washes, foliage/rock/water detail passes, atmospheric depth, distinct palettes per story location that still grade as one valley.
- **Hand-drawn motion** - true hand-drawn-feel performance: acting with weight and anticipation, lip/face timing on dialogue, walk/run/carry cycles with ground contact, cloth/hair secondary motion driven by the per-shot wind vector, 2s line boil retained and extended to face inks.
- **Dialogue, SFX, and score continuity** - a dialogue pass (script, per-line timing, caption/VTT sync rebuilt byte-exact), an SFX pass (footsteps, cloth, fire, water, storm, crowd/wind beds matched to the new cut), and a score re-cue (the existing leitmotif rows re-orchestrated onto the new beats with no dead air and no unmotivated entrances).
- **Trailer and posters** - a 30-second trailer re-cut from the new timeline plus poster refresh only where the new story demands it.
- **Reproducible pipeline, green throughout** - `film/tools/render.mjs`, `render-audio.mjs`, `render-captions.mjs`, `capture.mjs`, and `audit.mjs` plus `film/repro.sh` stay green on every phase PR; all sources committed; deterministic rebuilds byte-identical. The pipeline is extended (new gates), never broken.
- **Unified docs** - `film/README.md` plus `film/docs/` rewritten as one product view of the reimagined film; no milestone chapters, no `this milestone` language, no internal phase numbers in public docs.

## Why

The owner verdict is binding: #449 was good work but closed too soon, with a story that is hard to follow, too little action and drama, placeholder-grade figures, thin backgrounds, bare-minimum motion, and off-feeling dialogue delivery. This rebuild exists to fix exactly those six gaps with considerably more time and iteration than the first pass. The honest engineering path is the same one that made #449 shippable - a deterministic procedural hand-drawn engine driven by a real screenplay and a real score, iterated through the lab's own watch-and-critique loop - now aimed at a higher bar: the Evaluator weighs story legibility, character craft, continuity, and emotional impact, not just technical gates.

## How It Works

- **Deterministic heart (unchanged).** One seeded RNG (`film/engine/rng.js`, committed seed) drives every jitter, grain fleck, and musical ornament. Same seed plus same sources equals pixel-identical frames, verified by hash tests. No wall-clock, no network, no randomness leaks into the render path; the player clock is the timeline. The rebuild adds deterministic seeds per new subsystem (face boil, cloth sim, background detail) derived from the master seed, never from `Math.random` or `Date`.
- **Timeline stays the source of truth.** `film/story/screenplay.json` is replaced by the rewritten shot list (still 5 acts, 240-300 s total): each shot declares duration, camera, cast, background, dialogue/caption lines with per-line in/out times, music cues, SFX, and the new continuity fields (entry state, exit state, cause link to the previous shot). The engine remains a pure function of (timeline, time): scrubbable, pausable, testable. A continuity ledger tool verifies every shot's entry state matches the previous shot's exit state.
- **Replacement in place, pipeline never red.** The rebuild overwrites `film/story/`, `film/engine/`, `film/score/`, and `film/captions.vtt` incrementally across phases, keeping `film/index.html` and the player contract untouched until the final phase. Each phase PR must leave `bash film/repro.sh` green: when a source file is rewritten, its generators, tests, and manifests move in the same PR. No phase may land with a known-red audit.
- **Human-first character engine.** New `film/engine/humans.js` owns proportion-accurate body construction (head-unit model sheets, jointed limbs with FK poses, torso twist, ground-contact feet), `film/engine/faces.js` owns heads (skull, eyes with gaze + blink, brows, nose hint, mouth with a small phoneme set for dialogue beats, expression blender over the phoneme base), and `film/engine/acting.js` owns performance (per-shot acting beats: anticipation, action, reaction, hold; weight shifts; exertion tremor; secondary-motion drivers for cloak, hair, scarf fed by the shot's wind vector). Bodies, faces, and acting compose inside the existing `poseFor` rig contract so the 24 fps frame grid, scrub-exact renderer, and capture loop keep working.
- **Backgrounds as paintings, not tints.** `film/engine/backgrounds.js` is rebuilt location by location: each background gets a composition sketch (horizon, focal mass, light source), three wash layers (sky, mid, fore), a detail pass (foliage clusters, rock facets, water reflections, interior props), and an atmosphere pass (mist, ember light falloff, storm murk). Detail density scales down honestly at 390 px (fewer strokes, same composition) instead of blurring.
- **Dialogue as a timed performance.** Each caption line carries speaker, text, start/end on the frame lattice, and an emotion tag driving the face blender and the live audio ducking. `tools/render-captions.mjs` rebuilds `captions.vtt` byte-exact; the audit pins cue count, lattice alignment, speaker coverage, and VTT match. SFX events are re-anchored to the new dialogue and action beats; the mix enforces dialogue-first ducking (beds dip under lines, never over them).
- **Score re-cued, not discarded.** The four motif rows (Nia, wind, guardian ostinato, cairn hymn) survive; the cue map is rewritten onto the new beats with continuous coverage (no shot without a cue, no cue without a resolving voice) and the storm/set-piece forces from Phase 4 retained. The live WebAudio performer and the offline WAV stems render from the same event lists.
- **Theatre page (stable contract).** `film/index.html` keeps its 16:9 stage, transport controls, chapter menu, caption track, trailer mode, end card, storyboard wall, posters, loading/error/empty states, and 390 px responsive rules. Behavioural changes are additive only (e.g. expression-correct reduced-motion stills, new chapters matching the new acts).
- **Watch-and-iterate loop (binding).** `film/tools/capture.mjs` dumps per-shot hero frames plus new face close-up cards and background plates for self-critique; the Builder self-reviews stills every phase and tightens keyframes, timing, and mixes before pushing. The Tester plays the film end to end on desktop and 390 px; the Evaluator gates on craft (story legibility, character craft, continuity, emotional impact) before merge.

- `film/player/` - contract-stable: `player.js` (dialogue ducking + new chapters), `gallery.js` (new plates and close-up cards), `player.css` (only what the new staging needs), posters refreshed where demanded.
- `film/tools/` - `render.mjs` (stills + manifest), `render-audio.mjs` (WAV mix + stems), `render-captions.mjs` (VTT export with dialogue timing), `capture.mjs` (hero frames + face close-ups + background plates), `audit.mjs` (new gates: continuity ledger, dialogue lattice, human-rig coverage, background coverage, score continuity), all extended, none removed.

## Story Structure (binding runtime: 240-300 s; Builder finalizes beats, keeps this skeleton)

- Act 1, The Failure (0:00-0:50): Nia, keeper-in-training under grandmother keeper Yara, lets the Hollow's lantern fail on her watch through haste, not malice. Cause establishes guilt; exit state (dark lantern, Yara's withheld trust) is the engine of every later act. Action set-piece: the hillside extinguishing against the wind.
- Act 2, The Debt (0:50-1:50): Yara sets the price: carry one keeper flame to the high cairn before the pass closes, and take the stranded ferryman's children waiting at the gorge station as your charge. Nia resists, then accepts. Exit state: a charged errand with conflicting obligations (flame vs. children).
- Act 3, The Gorge (1:50-2:50): Valley crossing with the young ferryman (civil, frightened, competent at boats, useless on cliffs) and his injured younger sister (carried, observant, the film's truth-teller). Action set-pieces: washed-out bridge, rope traverse, rockfall. Midpoint turn: the children save Nia first, inverting the debt.
- Act 4, The Choice (2:50-3:50): Storm ascent with one flame and two needs: the sister's fever needs the flame's warmth now, the cairn needs it intact at the top. Nia spends half the flame to save the sister, climbing with a guttering stub. The wind turns from adversary to guide only after Nia pays a real price. Emotional peak played in close-up dialogue, not narration.
- Act 5, Hearthlight (3:50-4:30): The stub should not be enough, but the valley answers: every household that kept a spark through the dark (earned by Nia's station-by-station kindness in Act 3) raises it as she passes, and the cairn lights from many flames, Nia's least among them. Coda: Yara hands Nia the keeper's knot without a word; the sister ties the final ribbon. No narrator explains what the picture already says.

Continuity invariant (audit-enforced): every shot declares entry state (who is where, holding what, flame level, knowledge each character holds) and exit state; the next shot's entry must match. No teleporting props, no knowledge a character should not have, no weather that resets between cuts.

## Test Matrix

- Runtime gate: total timeline within 240-300 s; trailer 25-35 s.
- Story legibility: continuity ledger passes (entry equals previous exit for every shot); each act has exactly one goal; the choice in Act 4 and its payoff in Act 5 are present and ordered; a first-time-viewer caption-only read (captions without picture) still follows the plot.
- Character craft: every human lead matches its model-sheet proportions within tolerance; expression sheet coverage (joy, fear, grief, resolve, exhaustion) rendered and captured; turnaround symmetry verified; silhouettes readable at 390 px.
- Motion craft: dialogue beats have phoneme/face coverage; exertion beats show weight (crouch/lift/carry poses with ground contact); secondary motion present on cloth/hair per shot wind vector; line boil present on face inks.
- Dialogue/sound: VTT rebuild byte-matches; every cue lands on the frame lattice; every dialogue line has face coverage and ducks the beds; every shot has at least one music cue resolving to a performed voice; SFX cover every screenplay tag; master peak within soft-limit bounds with storm-to-quiet dynamic range.
- Determinism/provenance: hero-frame + close-up + plate hashes identical across runs; no image/audio binaries without committed generators; `bash film/repro.sh` green.
- Player: play/pause/seek/chapters/captions/fullscreen/volume verified live on desktop and 390 px; trailer mode follows the new map; error cards forced and screenshotted.
