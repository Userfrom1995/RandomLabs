# Mythduel - Original Thor vs Zeus Mythic Duel (Hand-Drawn-Style Fight Scene)

An original mythic duel fight scene at production quality, staged as a playable Pages film at `/mythduel/`: two original interpretations of public-domain myth figures, Thor (storm-bringer of the north) and Zeus (storm-lord of Olympus), meet over a contested storm and settle it in single combat. Deterministic real-time canvas render in a hand-drawn style through a reproducible in-repo pipeline (scripts plus sources plus deterministic build, no facade player, no stub frames), with an original synthesized score and SFX, chaptered beats, and a behind-the-scenes surface (designs, boards, pipeline docs). Same craft bar as the short-film commission: the Builder self-watches and iterates to production level; Reviewer plus Tester plus Evaluator gates before merge.

## IP guardrail (binding through every gate)

Thor and Zeus are built ONLY as original interpretations of public-domain Norse/Greek myth figures. No Marvel Thor likeness, costume, or story. No God of War / Sony Santa Monica Thor or Zeus likeness, design, audio, or script. No game or film assets of any kind. Original silhouettes, palettes, weapons, dialogue, choreography, score, and SFX throughout. Any PR introducing third-party likeness or assets fails review. Provenance is machine-enforced: every image and audio binary ships with a committed generator, and the audit carries an IP gate (forbidden-token scan plus asset-provenance check).

## Deliverables

Every player control works: play/pause, scrub/seek, chapters/beats, captions toggle, fullscreen, volume/mute, honest loading/error states, reduced-motion respect, 390 px mobile verified.
- **Original story and choreography** - cause of the clash, escalation, and resolution; beat-by-beat boards and animatic timing driving the timeline; per-beat continuity fields (stance, ground, weapon state, storm state, knowledge each fighter holds) so no teleporting weapons, no reset weather, no unreadable reversals.
- **Original character design** - two original fighter designs with distinct silhouettes, palettes, and weapon reads (northern hammer-haft vs Olympian bolt-shaft, both original shapes); model sheets (head-unit proportions), turnarounds, expression/effort sheets, costume and secondary-motion notes; committed under `mythduel/designs/`.
- **Hand-drawn-style animation engine** - frame-by-frame-feel production through code: proportion bodies with FK limbs, hands that grip, throw, and catch, faces with gaze, blink, brows, and effort mouths, weight-shifting footwork with ground contact, anticipation/action/reaction/hold acting beats, cloth/hair/fur secondary motion driven by the per-beat wind vector, 2s line boil on inks, painted arena backgrounds with wash layers and atmosphere, impact particles (sparks, spray, dust, storm) anchored to painted geography. 24 fps frame grid, scrubbable pure function of (timeline, time).
- **Presentation surface** - Pages entrypoint at `/mythduel/` (player, chapters/beats, trailer mode, end card, storyboard wall, posters), plus behind-the-scenes: designs, boards, pipeline docs under `mythduel/docs/`.
- **Original audio** - original score (duel motifs per fighter plus clash and resolution material, continuous coverage, no dead air, no unmotivated entrances) and original SFX (footfalls, cloth, weapon swings, impacts, storm beds matched to the cut), synthesized in-repo with committed generators; dialogue-first ducking where voiced beats exist; offline WAV stems plus live WebAudio performer rendering from the same event lists. No external streaming, no third-party tracks.
- **Reproducible pipeline, green throughout** - `mythduel/tools/` generators (frames, audio, captions, capture, audit) plus `mythduel/repro.sh` stay green on every phase PR; all sources committed; deterministic rebuilds byte-identical.
- **Unified docs** - `mythduel/README.md` plus `mythduel/docs/` as one product view; no milestone chapters, no `this milestone` language, no internal phase numbers in public docs.

## Why

The owner commission from Brainstorm Board #42 demands a studio-grade mythic duel with no hurry: every frame carefully rendered and designed. The honest engineering path is the one proven on the short-film builds: a deterministic procedural hand-drawn engine driven by a real story and a real score, iterated through the lab's own watch-and-critique loop (Builder self-review of stills, Tester end-to-end play, Evaluator craft gate). A fight scene adds one discipline the short film never stressed: combat legibility. Every exchange must read first-time-viewer clear: who advanced, who was hit, what it cost, how the storm answered. Spectacle without legibility fails the bar.

## How It Works

- **Deterministic heart.** One seeded RNG (committed master seed) drives every jitter, grain fleck, boil offset, and musical ornament. Same seed plus same sources equals pixel-identical frames, verified by hash tests. Deterministic per-subsystem streams (ink boil, cloth sim, background detail, particle fields) derive from the master seed, never from `Math.random` or `Date`. The player clock is the timeline.
- **Timeline as source of truth.** `mythduel/story/duel.json` declares the full bout: beats with duration, camera, cast staging, background, beat lines/captions with in/out times, music cues, SFX tags, and continuity fields (entry/exit state per fighter, weapon state, storm level, cause link to the previous beat). The engine is a pure function of (timeline, time): scrubbable, pausable, testable. A continuity tool verifies every beat's entry matches the previous beat's exit.
- **Replacement-safe incremental build.** The duel is built up across phases inside one `mythduel/` tree, keeping `bash mythduel/repro.sh` green on every phase PR: when a source file lands, its generators, tests, and manifests land in the same PR. No phase lands with a known-red audit. Early phases may stage simplified stand-in motion only where the progress file names it, and never as fake controls or stub frames presented as finished craft.
- **Fighter-first character engine.** `mythduel/engine/fighters.js` owns proportion bodies (head-unit sheets, jointed FK limbs, torso twist, ground-contact feet), `faces.js` owns heads (gaze plus blink, brows, effort mouths, exertion blender), `acting.js` owns combat performance (advance/retreat/circle, anticipation, strike, impact reaction, hold, grapple/clinch beats, exertion tremor, secondary-motion drivers for cloaks, beards, aegis-fringe fed by the beat wind vector). Bodies, faces, and acting compose inside one rig contract so the 24 fps grid, scrub-exact renderer, and capture loop keep working.
- **Arena as painting, not tint.** `mythduel/engine/arena.js` paints the duelling ground (a storm-cut high crag between northern sea-cliffs and pale Olympian marble, an original location belonging to neither myth): composition sketch (horizon, focal mass, light source), wash layers (sky, mid, fore), detail pass (rock facets, sea-foam, marble veins, storm murk), atmosphere pass (rain veils, ember/spark falloff). Detail budgets scale down honestly at 390 px (fewer strokes, same composition), never blurred.
- **Combat as timed performance.** Each beat carries its exchange script (footwork, strikes, blocks, hits, knockbacks) with per-exchange timing on the frame lattice; impact frames land SFX and score accents from the same event clock. `tools/render-captions.mjs` exports beat captions byte-exact; the audit pins cue count, lattice alignment, and coverage.
- **Score and SFX from one clock.** Fighter motifs (northern drum-and-horn-row vs Olympian string-and-bronze-row) plus clash ostinato and resolution hymn cover every beat with resolving voices. The live WebAudio performer and the offline WAV stems render from the same event lists; beds duck under voiced beats with measured floors.
- **Theatre page.** `mythduel/index.html` ships a 16:9 stage, transport, beat/chapter menu, caption track, trailer mode, end card, storyboard wall, posters, loading/error/empty states, and 390 px responsive rules. Behavioural additions stay additive.
- **Watch-and-iterate loop (binding).** `mythduel/tools/capture.mjs` dumps per-beat hero frames plus fighter close-up cards and arena plates for self-critique; the Builder self-reviews stills every phase and tightens keyframes, timing, and mixes before pushing. The Tester plays the duel end to end on desktop and 390 px; the Evaluator gates on craft (choreography legibility, character craft, continuity, impact feel, emotional resolution) before merge.

- `mythduel/designs/` - committed model sheets, turnarounds, expression/effort sheets, palette and silhouette plates, weapon design sheets (all original; SVG sources, no binary without generator).

- `mythduel/score/` - `orchestra.js` event spec (original motifs), `voices.js` (synth voices), `sfx.js` (original foley generators: footfalls, cloth, swings, impacts, storm), `mix.js` (dialogue-first ducking), `animatic-audio.js` (live performer), committed stems regenerated deterministically.
- `mythduel/player/` - `player.js` (transport plus beat chapters plus ducking), `gallery.js` (plates and close-up cards, storyboard wall), `player.css`, posters.
- `mythduel/tools/` - `render.mjs` (stills plus manifest), `render-audio.mjs` (WAV mix plus stems), `render-captions.mjs` (caption export), `capture.mjs` (hero frames plus fighter close-ups plus arena plates), `audit.mjs` (gates: continuity ledger, lattice, fighter-rig coverage, arena coverage, score continuity, IP/provenance), none removed once added.
- `mythduel/tests/` - suites for choreography legibility (one readable exchange per beat, advance/hit/cost ordering, choice-and-payoff presence), character craft (model-sheet proportions, effort coverage, turnaround symmetry, 390 px silhouette readability), motion craft (impact timing, ground contact, secondary motion per wind vector, line boil), sound (caption rebuild match, lattice alignment, cue coverage, ducking bounds, stem determinism), determinism/provenance (hero hashes identical across runs, no binary without generator, IP token scan), player (transport/chapters/captions/fullscreen/volume live on desktop and 390 px, trailer mode, error cards).

## Story Skeleton (binding; Builder finalizes beats, keeps this shape)

- Cause of the clash: a free-roaming storm neither fighter called gathers over the strait between their far shores; both storm-lords claim the right to loose it, neither will yield the sky. They meet by challenged consent on the storm-crag, with terms spoken before weapons rise.
- Escalation: ranging exchanges (wind and footwork), champions' exchanges (thrown weapons recalled, body blows, knockdowns), storm's answer (the sky turns on both fighters and must be weathered together for one beat), final exchange (one clean opening, one withheld killing blow).
- Resolution: mercy as strength. The fighter with the opening stays the blow, names the cost, and both loose the storm out to sea together. No narrator explains what the picture already says.

Continuity invariant (audit-enforced): every beat declares entry state (who stands where, holding what, wounds and fatigue each fighter carries, storm level, what each fighter knows) and exit state; the next beat's entry must match. No teleporting weapons, no forgotten wounds, no weather that resets between cuts.

## Test Matrix

- Runtime gate: full duel within 150-200 s; trailer 25-35 s.
- Choreography legibility: continuity ledger passes (entry equals previous exit for every beat); each beat has exactly one readable exchange (advance, hit, cost); a first-time-viewer caption-only read still follows the bout.
- Character craft: every fighter matches its model-sheet proportions within tolerance; effort-sheet coverage rendered and captured; turnaround symmetry verified; silhouettes readable at 390 px; weapon reads distinct at both widths.
- Motion craft: impact beats land SFX and score accents on the lattice; exertion shows weight (crouch/lift/knockback poses with ground contact); secondary motion present on cloth/hair per beat wind vector; line boil present on inks.
- Sound: caption rebuild byte-matches; every cue lands on the frame lattice; voiced beats duck the beds; every beat has at least one music cue resolving to a performed voice; SFX cover every choreography tag; master peak within soft-limit bounds with storm-to-quiet dynamic range.
- Determinism/provenance/IP: hero-frame plus close-up plus plate hashes identical across runs; no image/audio binaries without committed generators; `bash mythduel/repro.sh` green; forbidden-likeness token scan clean.
- Player: play/pause/seek/chapters/captions/fullscreen/volume verified live on desktop and 390 px; trailer mode follows the map; error cards forced and screenshotted.
