coding with realistic maps retains too little of its oracle margin to carry

B1 harvest to first-class composition candidate under fresh pre-registered

## Why

V1 bought three durable facts, not just a rejection: the static spine is
real and already exceeds the research's realistic B1 band; spatial maps and
trees cost more than they explain at legal caps; HYB tokenizations lose to
zero-flag-first everywhere. The remaining open buckets (B2 conditioning,
B3 predictors, B4 trials) are all SOURCE-side mechanisms a decoder can
replicate causally - exactly what "source-side-only" means - and their
midpoint sum plus the measured spine clears M2 comfortably while M3 stays
honestly unlikely without near-edge outcomes or the S5 reserve.

## How It Works

Every phase scores each residual stream in TWO frames against pinned
controls: FRAME-A (production adaptive replay, the e1-equivalent instrument)
answers what the shipped coder gains; FRAME-S (static spine, all side-info
netted) is PRIMARY because composition ships where mechanisms win. S1 runs
the addendum 18.4 predictor mathematics verbatim (GAP reduced, W ensemble
16.16); S2 gives bias feedback its one sanctioned canary on an S1 PASS;
S3 hashes the frozen causal property vector P_ext into K <= 256 clusters
with no spatial maps anywhere; S4 composes survivors per image by real NET
bytes (adaptive control included, so composed NET cannot regress vs e1 by
construction) crossed with D4c color trials, then projects the corpus via
the 18.5 formula against the committed e1 CSV. Threshold PASS hands to a
fresh format-program blueprint; FAIL stop-and-reports with the full ledger.
All six VB rails stay green per slice; dated one-file-per-phase CSVs;
pins verified before any run; zero container bytes until S4 passes.
