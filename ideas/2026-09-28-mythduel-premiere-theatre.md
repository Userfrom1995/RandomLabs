# 2026-09-28 - Mythduel Phase 5 - Premiere Theatre and Behind-the-Scenes

Phase 5 of the Mythduel epic (issue #470): the premiere surface over the
finished duel. A 30-second trailer cut plays in the theatre through the same
stage paint (trailer frames match duel frames pixel for pixel), both cuts
close on an end card with credits and replay, three deterministic posters
hang on the entrypoint, the behind-the-scenes docs link the full pipeline,
and the root landing points at the duel.

## What was built

- `mythduel/engine/trailer.js`: pure trailer-clock to duel-time map over
  `story/trailer.json` (7 windows, 30 s total, every edge on the 24 fps
  lattice inside its beat). `trailerCutAt` maps any trailer instant onto the
  exact duel second to paint, clamped past the end.
- Trailer mode in `mythduel/player/player.js`: the Trailer button (or T)
  re-ranges the seek bar onto the 30 s clock, paints through `paintStage`,
  follows cuts with the live score cue map, names each cut in the caption
  band, and highlights the cut beat in the chapter menu. Chapter jumps and
  restart exit trailer mode back to the full duel.
- End card (`#endCard`): original credits (story, designs, choreography,
  score, SFX all created for this film; public-domain myth only), Watch
  again and Watch trailer buttons. Shown at the end of both cuts.
- `mythduel/tools/render-posters.mjs` plus `mythduel/designs/posters/`: key
  art plus Thor and Zeus fighter sheets, painted deterministically from the
  duel seed (skyburst fork, seeded rain and grain, original hooded-figure
  marks with haft-hammer and shaft-bolt). Rebuild is byte-identical.
- Fallbacks verified: loading veil, error overlay, noscript note, null 2D
  context guard with an actionable message, reduced-motion held stills
  (trailer clock steps by seek), 390 px mobile CSS pass.
- Audit 48 to 56 gates, `mythduel/tests/phase5-premiere.mjs` (46 probes),
  `repro.sh` extended with the premiere suite and the poster check.

## Notes

- The trailer reuses the duel paint path rather than a second renderer: one
  stage, two clocks. Frame parity is machine-checked by sampling the trailer
  clock every 0.5 s plus cut edges.
- Posters are committed SVGs (text, provenance-clean) regenerated by a
  committed tool, the same pattern as the committed JSON audio stems.
