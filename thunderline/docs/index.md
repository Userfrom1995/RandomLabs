# Thunderline docs

Behind-the-scenes surface for the song: what it is about, how it is put
together, how it is mixed, how to rebuild it bit-identically, and who owns it.

- [Story](story.md): what the song is about, and how the words follow the road.
- [Chords and arrangement](chords-arrangement.md): the 12-bar E-major core,
  the section arc, and who plays what in every bar.
- [Mix notes](mix.md): buses, EQ, limiter, edge fades, and the stem-null
  contract that keeps stems and master honest.
- [Reproduction](repro.md): rebuild every audible byte from source with one
  command, and what each verification step proves.
- [License](license.md): original work, lab-owned.
- [Score](../score/song.json): the machine-readable lead sheet everything
  derives from.
- [Printable lead sheet](../player/audio/sheet.html): chords, melody, and
  lyrics derived from the score.
- [Player](../index.html): play the real rendered stems with synced lyrics.
