Behind the scenes

Story, chords, mix, reproduction, and license for an original rock-and-roll song.

Story

A night drive that ends at sunrise: midnight interstate, diner coffee and a jukebox, the no-exit stretch under the lead break, dawn over the pines, and the throttle held open into the morning light. The hook is the title line: Ridin' on the thunderline. Read the story

Chords and arrangement

A 160 BPM E-major 12-bar core (E7-A7-B7) across 104 bars and 156 seconds: intro, three verses, two choruses, a lead break, a lifted 16-bar final chorus, and a sung outro tag. Every bar carries rhythm plus bass plus a front voice. Read the chord and arrangement notes

Mix

Four stem buses (vocals, guitars, bass, drums) through per-bus EQ into a soft-knee limiter at the 0.89 ceiling, with pre-limiter edge fades so the track never clicks. Stems null against the master within 0.02 RMS (shipped: 0.0014). Read the mix notes

Reproduction

One command rebuilds every audible byte from the score with pinned stdlib-only tooling and verifies it: bash thunderline/repro.sh (score export, render, double-render bit identity, 12-check audit, full suite). Read the reproduction steps

License

Original work, lab-owned: every note, word, and byte was authored in-repo. Read the license
