## Goal

Produce an original 4-5 minute hand-drawn-style animated short film,
"Hearthlight", with an original old-style orchestral score, premiered on
Pages at `/film/` through a reproducible in-repo pipeline. Binding gates:
runtime 240-300 s, coherent original story/characters/script, hand-drawn
feel, original score + sound, desktop + 390 px playback verified, review +
test + eval green, unified docs.
