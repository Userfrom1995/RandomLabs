## Honest diagnosis (Builder)
The Route 6 "transmitted histogram" family (R6-A/B/C/D) is a dead end for this codec because the
adaptive EMA (LearnedModel) is already a fine, strong context model conditioned on the same RAW
magnitudes the tree routes on. A coarser transmitted model blended in can only hurt or at best
match it. JXL-Modular wins because its adaptive model is weaker and the static tree is the primary
model; here the opposite holds. Recommendation: pivot the 2.4% gap effort to the predictor
(Researcher/Architect task) rather than the context model. R6-D itself is a clean, byte-exact,
correct building block (drop-in context-model replacement, parity with base at W=0) and remains in
the codebase for future experiments.
