8. Fairness audit (structural, pre-sweep pass complete)

  • One harness, identical adapter contract: same timeouts, warmup, JSON schema, failure semantics. Adapters differ only in config_text() and port. Contract: docs/harness-contract.md.
  • Same runner image, same PG build and config, freshly initialized dataset per run, interleaved round-robin order, medians over repeats, per-chunk direct control.
  • Every non-default pooler setting cites that pooler's own tuning docs in docs/configs/; anything uncited fails review. Full re-check: docs/fairness-audit.md.
  • Budget parity published: M1 has 7 workloads per pooler; M2 realized rows are pgagroal 11, PgBouncer 12, Odyssey 11, pgcat 9, pgpool-II 9 (structural spread, documented, favors nobody). N/A rows committed as nulls.
  • How results are computed, compared, and exported: docs/results.md (medians, binding gate, CSV/JSON schemas).
  • Anti-theater checklist enforced: no home-team tuning, no sabotage by omission, no cherry-picked workloads, no hidden failures, no interpolated N/A, no carried-forward numbers across upgrades. The Tester independently reproduces at least one sample cell before any gate passes.

9. Threats to validity

  1. Co-location: PG, pooler, and pgbench share 2-4 vCPUs; pgbench itself can bottleneck. Results rank arms on this hardware only, never absolute capacity claims.
  2. Prepared-mode incompatibility: transaction pooling breaks server-side prepares unless the pooler tracks them; errors are findings, not harness bugs; every prepared cell has a simple-protocol twin.
  3. Auth and startup asymmetry: -C cells measure connect plus auth; reset queries and auth methods are held constant and logged.
  4. Session-state leakage: custom scripts stay transaction-scoped; builtins already do.
  5. Reset-query cost: DISCARD ALL-style resets are part of the fair cost of multiplexing, held constant and reported.
  6. Autovacuum and checkpoints: fixed CHECKPOINT, identical vacuum defaults, warmup trimming via aggregate-interval logs.
  7. Shared-runner noise: handled by interleaving and medians, never by static baselines or single runs.
  8. pgpool-II session-class-only comparison is labeled, not hidden; Odyssey statement results are labeled provisional until empirically verified.

Full text: docs/methodology.md sections 6-7.
