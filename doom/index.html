Controls

Desktop: WASD or arrows move and turn, mouse look after clicking the canvas (Pointer Lock), Space or left click fires. Touch devices show an on-screen stick plus FIRE, USE, strafe, weapon, and menu buttons. Every key remaps below.

    Milestone 2 scope

    WebGL paletted renderer (Tier 0) with RGBA fallback (Tier 1) and Canvas2D fallback (Tier 2/3), resolution ladder with battery saver, 35 Hz ticcmd pipeline with Pointer Lock mouse look, touch overlay with stick plus strafe plus weapon strip, and persisted remapping. Audio plus persistence (M3) and ecosystem polish (M4) are future milestones.
